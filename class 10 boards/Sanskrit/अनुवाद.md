# 📝 Sanskrit Anuvad (Translation) - Rapid Revision Notes

**Source:** Master Sahab - Class 10th Sanskrit (Rapid Revision)
**Topic:** Anuvad (Translation from Hindi to Sanskrit)

## 1. The Foundation: Lakar (Tenses/Moods)
Lakar is the Sanskrit equivalent of Tense. Identifying the correct Lakar from the Hindi sentence is the very first step in translation.

* **Lat Lakar (लट् लकार):** Present Tense
  * *Identification:* sentence ends in "ता है", "ती है", "रहा है" (e.g., राहुल जाता है - Rahul goes).
* **Lang Lakar (लङ् लकार):** Past Tense
  * *Identification:* sentence ends in "था", "थी", "थे" (e.g., राहुल जाता था - Rahul went).
* **Lrit Lakar (लृट् लकार):** Future Tense
  * *Identification:* sentence ends in "गा", "गी", "गे" (e.g., राहुल जाएगा - Rahul will go).
* **Lot Lakar (लोट् लकार):** Imperative Mood (Order/Request/Blessing)
  * *Identification:* commanding tone (e.g., राहुल तुम जाओ! - Rahul, you go!).
* **Vidhilin Lakar (विधिलिङ् लकार):** Potential Mood (Should/Can)
  * *Identification:* sentence contains "चाहिए" or "सकना" (e.g., राहुल को पढ़ना चाहिए - Rahul should read).

## 2. Purush & Vachan (Person & Number)
Verbs (Kriya) must *always* match the Subject (Karta) in Purush and Vachan.

### Purush (Person) Chart
| Person                   | Sanskrit (Singular / Dual / Plural) |                |               | Hindi Meaning                                       |
| :----------------------- | :---------------------------------- | -------------- | ------------- | :-------------------------------------------------- |
| **Uttam Purush (1st)**   | अहम् (Aham) / वयम् (Vayam)          | आवाम् (Aawam)  | वयम् (Vayam)  | मैं (I) / हम दोनों (We two) / हम लोग (We all)       |
| **Madhyam Purush (2nd)** | त्वम् (Twam) / यूयम् (Yuyam)        | युवाम् (Yuvam) | यूयम् (Yuyam) | तुम (You) / तुम दोनों (You two) / तुम लोग (You all) |
| **Pratham Purush (3rd)** | स: / सा (He/She)                    | तौ / ते        |               | All other subjects (He, She, It, Names, Nouns).     |


*Note: Uttam and Madhyam Purush are gender-neutral. Pratham Purush changes based on gender (e.g., स: for boy, सा for girl).*

## 3. The Karak Formula (Standard Cases)
Determine the role of each word to apply the correct Vibhakti (Case ending based on Shabd Roop like Balak, Lata, Phal).

| Karak (Case Role) | Hindi Marker (चिह्न) | Meaning / Role | Vibhakti to Apply |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Karta (Subject)** | ने (Ne) | The one doing the action. | **Prathama (1st)** |
| **Karma (Object)** | को (Ko) | What the action is being done to. | **Dwitiya (2nd)** |
| **Karan (Instrument)** | से / के द्वारा (Se/Dwara) | The tool/helper used for the action (No separation). | **Tritiya (3rd)** |
| **Sampradan (Dative)**| को / के लिए (Ko/Ke liye) | For whom something is given/done. | **Chaturthi (4th)** |
| **Apadan (Ablative)** | से (Se - Alagav) | Separation (e.g., leaves falling from a tree). | **Panchami (5th)** |
| **Sambandh (Genitive)**| का, के, की (Ka/Ke/Ki) | Shows possession or relationship. | **Shashti (6th)** |
| **Adhikaran (Locative)**| में, पर (Me/Par) | The base/location of the action. | **Saptami (7th)** |

### Example Breakdowns by Karak:
* **Karma:** राहुल विद्यालय जाता है। (Rahul goes to school)
  * *Analysis:* "School" is the object (Where is he actually going?).
  * *Translation:* राहुल: (Prathama) **विद्यालयम् (Dwitiya)** गच्छति।
* **Karan:** मोहन वायुयान से जाता है। (Mohan goes by airplane)
  * *Analysis:* "Airplane" is the helper tool (no separation).
  * *Translation:* मोहन: (Prathama) **वायुयानेन (Tritiya)** गच्छति।
* **Sampradan:** (वह) मोहन के लिए पुस्तक देता है। (He gives a book for Mohan)
  * *Analysis:* Mohan is receiving the book (के लिए).
  * *Translation:* स: **मोहनाय (Chaturthi)** पुस्तकं ददाति।
* **Apadan:** वृक्ष से पत्ते गिरते हैं। (Leaves fall from the tree)
  * *Analysis:* Leaves are separating from the tree.
  * *Translation:* **वृक्षात् (Panchami)** पत्राणि पतन्ति।
* **Adhikaran:** राहुल विद्यालय में जाता है। (Rahul goes in the school)
  * *Analysis:* School is the base location ("में" is present).
  * *Translation:* राहुल: **विद्यालये (Saptami)** गच्छति।

## 4. Upapad Vibhakti (The Exception Rules) 🚨
**Crucial Concept:** Upapad Vibhakti words override the standard Karak rules. If these specific words appear in a sentence, you **must** use their assigned Vibhakti, ignoring the Hindi markers (like का/के/की).

### Dwitiya Vibhakti Exceptions
If you see these words, apply Dwitiya to the preceding noun:
* **उभयत: / अभित:** (Both sides)
* **परित:** (Around / All four sides)
* **सर्वत:** (Everywhere / All sides)
* **समया / निकषा** (Near / Close to)
* **प्रति** (Towards)

*Example:* गांव के दोनों ओर वृक्ष हैं। (There are trees on both sides of the village.)
* *Normal logic:* गांव **के** = Shashti (Gramasya).
* *Upapad rule:* "दोनों ओर" (Abhitah) forces Dwitiya!
* *Correct translation:* **ग्रामम् (Dwitiya)** अभित: वृक्षा: सन्ति।

### Tritiya Vibhakti Exceptions
If you see these words, apply Tritiya:
* **सह / समम्** (With / Along with)
* **विना** (Without - can also take 2nd or 5th)

### Panchami Vibhakti Exceptions
If you see this word, apply Panchami:
* **बहि:** (Outside)

*Example (CBSE Favorite):* घर के बाहर बच्चे खेल रहे हैं। (Children are playing outside the house.)
* *Normal logic:* घर **के** = Shashti (Grihasya).
* *Upapad rule:* "बाहर" (Bahih) forces Panchami!
* *Correct translation:* **गृहात् (Panchami)** बहि: बालका: क्रीडन्ति।

**💡 Master Sahab's Pro-Tips for the Exam:**
1. **Verb matches Subject:** If the subject is Singular/Pratham Purush, the verb must be Singular/Pratham Purush. (They dance together!).
2. **Look for English clues:** If you are confused about whether a Hindi noun is singular or plural (e.g., "पत्ते" vs "पत्ता" or "फूल"), glance at the English translation provided in the CBSE paper (e.g., "Tree" vs "Trees") to determine the Vachan. 
3. **Use of 'To be' (है):** 
   * Singular: अस्ति (Asti)
   * Dual: स्तः (Stah)
   * Plural: सन्ति (Santi - e.g., वृक्षा: सन्ति)
