## Marking Scheme (2024-25)

**Total Marks:** 100 (Theory: 80 | Internal Assessment: 20)

**Time Allowed:** 3 Hours

The communicative approach to teaching Sanskrit focuses on linguistic competence, practical usage, and comprehension. The theory question paper is divided into four main sections.

### 📝 Detailed Syllabus Breakdown

### **Section A: Reading Comprehension (अपठित-अवबोधनम्) - 10 Marks**

This section tests the student's ability to read and comprehend an unseen Sanskrit passage (approx. 80-100 words).

- **One-word answers** (एकपदेन उत्तरत) - 2 Marks
    
- **Full-sentence answers** (पूर्णवाक्येन उत्तरत) - 4 Marks
    
- **Appropriate Title** (शीर्षकलेखनम्) - 1 Mark
    
- **Linguistic / Grammar-based questions** (भाषिककार्यम्) - 3 Marks _(Questions on noun-adjective, verb-subject, synonyms/antonyms, or pronoun reference)._
    

### **Section B: Writing Skills (रचनात्मक-कार्यम्) - 15 Marks**

This section tests the creative and functional writing abilities of the student using given hints (Manjusha/मञ्जूषा).

1. **Informal / Formal Letter (पत्र-लेखनम्):** Fill in the blanks in a provided letter using the clue box. (5 Marks)
    
2. **Picture Composition or Paragraph Writing (चित्र-वर्णनम् / अनुच्छेद-लेखनम्):** Write 5 simple Sanskrit sentences describing a given picture OR writing a short paragraph on a specific topic using hints. (5 Marks)
    
3. **Translation (अनुवादः):** Translate 5 simple sentences from English/Hindi to Sanskrit. (5 Marks)
    

### **Section C: Applied Grammar (अनुप्रयुक्त-व्याकरणम्) - 25 Marks**

Grammar is tested in context, largely based on the prescribed workbook (_Manika Abhyas Pustakam_).

- **Sandhi (सन्धि-कार्यम्):** Swar, Vyanjan, and Visarga Sandhi (4 Marks)
    
- **Samas (समासः):** Tatpurusha, Dvandva, Avyayibhava, and Bahuvrihi (4 Marks)
    
- **Pratyaya (प्रत्ययाः):** Taddhita and Kridanta affixes (4 Marks)
    
- **Vachya (वाच्य-परिवर्तनम्):** Active / Passive voice in present tense (3 Marks)
    
- **Time Writing (समय-लेखनम् / घटिका):** Stating time in Sanskrit (4 Marks)
    
- **Avyaya (अव्यय-पदानि):** Fill in the blanks with correct indeclinables (3 Marks)
    
- **Error Correction (अशुद्धि-संशोधनम्):** Correcting gender, case, verb, or number errors in sentences (3 Marks)
    

### **Section D: Literature (पठित-अवबोधनम्) - 30 Marks**

This section is based entirely on the prescribed textbook.

1. **Seen Passages for Comprehension (15 Marks):**
    
    - Prose passage (गद्यांश) - 5 Marks
        
    - Poetry/Shloka (पद्यांश) - 5 Marks
        
    - Drama/Play (नाट्यांश) - 5 Marks _(Each includes one-word, full-sentence, and linguistic questions)._
        
2. **Question Framing (प्रश्न-निर्माणम्):** Forming questions for given statements based on underlined words. (4 Marks)
    
3. **Anvaya / Bhavarth (अन्वयः / भावार्थः):** Completing the logical prose order of a Shloka or explaining its meaning using hints. (4 Marks)
    
4. **Event Sequencing (कथाक्रम-संयोजनम्):** Rearranging jumbled sentences of a story in the correct sequential order. (4 Marks)
    
5. **Word Meanings (शब्दार्थ-मेलनम्):** Matching words from the text with their correct contextual meanings/synonyms. (3 Marks)
    

### 📚 Prescribed Books

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) prescribes the following books for Subject Code 119:

1. **मणिका - द्वितीयो भागः (Manika - Part 2):** Main Textbook for Class 10 (Published by CBSE).
    
2. **CBSE Xcellence 95+ sanskrit manika + vayakarn** Grammar and Practice Workbook for Class 10 (Published by oswaal master sahab).
    

_Note: Students should thoroughly practice the 'Manjusha' (clue boxes) exercises from the Abhyas Pustakam, as Sections B and C rely heavily on this format._
